Upload Liver PET-CT Scan

Please upload a liver CT scan image for tumor detection and segmentation.

Supported formats: .png, .jpg, .jpeg

Instructions

  • Upload only a liver CT scan.
  • Do not upload normal photographs or unrelated images.
  • The system uses a SANet segmentation model for liver tumor segmentation.
  • The model is trained using PyTorch.
  • The federated training framework uses Flower.
  • Patient images remain within the local training environment during federated training.
